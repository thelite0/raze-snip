# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through issues and pull requests. Describe the
behavior being changed and how it was checked on a Wayland session.

By submitting a contribution for inclusion, you offer your contribution under
the [Raze Snip License 1.0](LICENSE), including its patent grant. You retain
ownership of your contribution. Only submit material you have the right to
license on these terms. Identify separately licensed third-party material and
its applicable license before adding it.

Build and run the geometry tests as documented in the README. Screenshot smoke
tests contain desktop pixels: keep those images and reports out of commits.
